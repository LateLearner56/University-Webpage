Why this page has been created

This has been a important part of my life, it was a interest I shared with my late Father and especially the airshows we shared together. An interest that has followed from childhood to adulthood, understanding we are here with the actions of the few who risked their lives so we can live ours. I live in part of the country where the Lancaster bomber had a heavy presence and still does with the restoration being applied. More on that later in the stories page. We need to remember that it is with the assistance of other european countries such as Poland who provided the pilots to allow the aircraft to be flown in the sorties.

My Passion for this topic

Although I have covered the aircraft for both the opposiung and defending the respective countries, you will notice I have special respect for Lancaster Bomber as the heavy bomber and with the escort of the spitfire and hurricanes provided a succesful resolution. It came a high price with the number of aricraft that never made it home and pilots either MIA or taken prisioner.
